Selected case study

From research to advocacy

#TheChoiceIsOurs translated KAP evidence on child, early and forced marriage in Kaduna State into targeted, culturally resonant communication.

STRATEGYCOMMUNICATIONINFLUENCE
01 / CASE STUDY

Turning complex issues into clearer narratives, stronger engagement and strategic action.

01 / Context & challenge

How can communication bridge the gap between awareness and the social, economic and power structures that sustain harmful practices?

The KAP study reported 89.7% awareness of health risks, while poverty, patriarchal decision-making, legal illiteracy and cultural norms remained drivers of the practice.

02 / My contribution

Where I contributed

My work focused on translating research into audience-centred advocacy communication, including segmentation of traditional and religious leaders, fathers, mothers, young men, peer CSOs and policymakers, and shaping accessible social media formats.

03 / Project evidence

What the project record tells us.

The evidence below is presented at project level unless explicitly identified as my own work.

  • KAP evidence reported 89.7% awareness of health risks
  • Campaign audiences included traditional and religious leaders, parents, young men, peer CSOs and policymakers
  • Communication formats were designed to make the evidence accessible to distinct audiences

The 89.7% figure is a KAP study finding, not a campaign-attributed change.
