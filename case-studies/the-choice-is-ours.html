From Research to Advocacy

#TheChoiceIsOurs translated KAP evidence on child, early and forced marriage in Kaduna State into targeted, culturally resonant communication.

The evidence

Knowledge did not automatically translate into changed practice.

The KAP study reported 89.7% awareness of health risks, while poverty, patriarchal decision-making, legal illiteracy and cultural norms remained drivers of practice.

Approach

Audience segmentation became the bridge between evidence and advocacy.

The campaign targeted traditional and religious leaders, fathers, mothers, young men, peer CSOs and policymakers using accessible social media formats.
