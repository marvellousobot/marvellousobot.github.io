Reaching People Excluded From Systems

LINKS combined community capacity strengthening, partnerships, digital outreach and service connections to improve access to inclusive SRHR information and support.

The challenge

How can communication reduce barriers for people who experience stigma, cost and exclusion?

The project worked with community organisations, healthcare workers, peer educators and partners.

Evidence

Communication connected people to knowledge, support and partnerships.

The project reports 20 trained community leaders, 5 trained healthcare workers, a 30% increase in DIVA Hotline engagement, 650+ individuals served through counselling/referrals, 18 peer educators trained and 822 direct beneficiaries.
