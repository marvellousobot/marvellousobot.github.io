Selected case study

Changing the conversation on abortion rights

Move2Act used coordinated advocacy, stakeholder engagement and Value Clarification and Attitude Transformation to strengthen the environment for improved abortion laws and policies in Nigeria.

STRATEGYCOMMUNICATIONINFLUENCE
01 / CASE STUDY

Turning complex issues into clearer narratives, stronger engagement and strategic action.

01 / Context & challenge

How do you shift a difficult policy conversation and strengthen the conditions for evidence-informed decision-making?

The project worked across Lagos and Imo, engaging government agencies, civil society and media in a challenging environment shaped by misinformation, stigma and institutional resistance.

02 / My contribution

Where I contributed

My contribution included facilitating the national dialogue on inclusive SRHR in Nigeria and supporting movement- and narrative-oriented engagement. This work helped create space for stakeholders to examine evidence, attitudes and policy priorities.

03 / Project evidence

What the project record tells us.

The evidence below is presented at project level unless explicitly identified as my own work.

  • Understanding abortion as safe when performed by trained providers increased from 60% to 86.7%
  • Awareness that legal restrictions do not reduce abortion rates increased from 55% to 80%
  • 9 MDAs and 10 CSOs engaged

These are project-level findings and engagement figures. They should not be interpreted as impact attributable to one facilitator or communicator alone.
