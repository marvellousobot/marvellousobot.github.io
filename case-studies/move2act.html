Changing the Conversation on Abortion Rights

Move2Act used coordinated advocacy, stakeholder engagement and Value Clarification and Attitude Transformation to strengthen the environment for improved abortion laws and policies in Nigeria.

The challenge

How do you shift a difficult policy conversation when misinformation, stigma and institutional resistance are part of the environment?

The project worked across Lagos and Imo, engaging government agencies, civil society and media.

Evidence

Measurable shifts in knowledge and attitudes.

Understanding abortion as safe when performed by trained providers rose from 60% to 86.7%; awareness that legal restrictions do not reduce abortion rates rose from 55% to 80%. The project also reported collaboration among 9 MDAs and 10 CSOs.
