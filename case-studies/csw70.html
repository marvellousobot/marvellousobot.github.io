From National Evidence to International Policy Advocacy

An organisational submission to CSW70 translating Nigerian evidence and experience into recommendations on access to justice for women and girls.

The challenge

How can national evidence become clear recommendations for international policy discussion?

The submission addressed legal, institutional, service, disability and digital-security barriers affecting access to justice.

Evidence

A structured policy agenda.

The submission proposed harmonised legal frameworks, gender- and disability-responsive justice institutions, an access-to-justice fund, gender-responsive budgeting and stronger protection for women’s rights defenders.
