From national evidence to international policy advocacy

Translating Nigerian evidence and experience into recommendations for the Commission on the Status of Women’s 70th session (CSW70).

STRATEGYCOMMUNICATIONINFLUENCE
01 / CASE STUDY

Turning complex issues into clearer narratives, stronger engagement and strategic action.

01 / Context & challenge

How can national evidence become clear, actionable recommendations for international policy discussion?

The submission addressed legal, institutional, service, disability and digital-security barriers affecting access to justice for women and girls.

02 / My contribution

Where I contributed

The portfolio work centres on shaping an organisational submission that translates context and evidence into a structured policy agenda. The available project record supports the submission’s subject matter and recommendations; it does not specify sole authorship or the precise division of drafting responsibilities.

03 / Project evidence

What the project record tells us.

The evidence below is presented at project level unless explicitly identified as my own work.

  • Harmonised legal frameworks
  • Gender- and disability-responsive justice institutions
  • An access-to-justice fund and gender-responsive budgeting
  • Stronger protection for women’s rights defenders

Recommendations documented in the submission; no claim is made here that they were adopted by CSW70.
