FACILITATION · ADVOCACY STRATEGY · SRHR

Activating Choices (AC) PAMOJA · Funded by AmplifyChange

From advocacy capacity to a stronger movement strategy.

A two-day capacity-strengthening workshop for the Pink Movement, a Lagos-based coalition of more than 15 organisations advancing sexual and reproductive health and rights.

THE CONTEXT

Strengthening a coalition's approach to policy influence.

The Pink Movement brings together more than 15 organisations working to advance SRHR in Lagos State, with a particular focus on reinstating the STOP Guidelines.

MY CONTRIBUTION

Training, strategic diagnosis and collective planning.

I facilitated a two-day capacity-strengthening workshop on effective communication and advocacy. Beyond delivering the training, I led a SWOT analysis of the coalition's existing advocacy strategy, helping members identify strategic strengths, gaps, opportunities and risks.

I also facilitated the development and validation of a strengthened advocacy strategy to provide clearer direction for the coalition's policy influence efforts.

WHAT THE ENGAGEMENT COMBINED

Capacity strengthening

Practical learning on effective communication and advocacy.

Strategic assessment

A participatory SWOT analysis of the coalition's existing advocacy approach.

Strategy development

Facilitation of a stronger advocacy strategy and its validation by coalition members.

CAPABILITIES DEMONSTRATED

Advocacy strategy development · Coalition and movement strengthening · Strategic communication training · Facilitation · SRHR policy influence.

This case study describes the workshop and strategy-development contribution. It does not claim that the STOP Guidelines were reinstated as a result of the engagement.
