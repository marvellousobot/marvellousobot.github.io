12,000+ Families and the Business of Sanitation

A sanitation marketing story spanning Ado, Ohimini and Igbo-Etiti LGAs.

What changed

A sanitation marketing programme connected household demand, local businesses, community leadership and communication.

The programme targeted improved sanitation access for over 12,000 families and used town halls, traditional-leader engagement, branding, promotional materials and jingles in Idoma and Igbo.

Communication operated as part of a broader development system.

The story demonstrates development communication, multilingual audience engagement, stakeholder mobilisation and impact documentation.
