FIELD NOTE / IMPACT

12,000+ families and the business of sanitation

A sanitation marketing story spanning Ado, Ohimini and Igbo-Etiti LGAs.

THE HUMAN STORY

Development work becomes more visible when we understand the people, systems and decisions behind it.

01 / The context

The programme connected household demand, local businesses, community leadership and communication to improve sanitation access.

The programme targeted improved sanitation access for over 12,000 families and used town halls, traditional-leader engagement, branding, promotional materials and jingles in Idoma and Igbo.

02 / Project evidence

  • 12,000+ families targeted
  • Town halls and traditional-leader engagement
  • Branding and promotional materials
  • Jingles in Idoma and Igbo

12,000+ is a programme target, not a verified number of households reached or a result attributable solely to my work.

03 / My contribution

Making the work visible and meaningful.

My communications contribution is represented by the use of community-facing communication, multilingual messaging and stakeholder mobilisation as part of the broader sanitation marketing approach. The available record does not isolate individual ownership of each activity or the programme-wide target.

04 / What this demonstrates

Communication that connects people, evidence and change.
