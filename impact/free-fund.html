From Cutting to Creating Livelihoods

A story from the FREE Fund project on FGM/C, livelihoods and community-level change.

What changed

Research connected the persistence of FGM/C to cultural norms and economic incentives, informing an alternative-livelihood response.

The project supported vocational training for former cutters, including soap-making and pastries training, alongside certificates and seed materials.

Evidence, social-norms analysis and human-centred communication.

The story demonstrates experience communicating complex gender and development issues with sensitivity and context.
