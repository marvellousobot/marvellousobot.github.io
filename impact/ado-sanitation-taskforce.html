When Advocacy Becomes Institutional Action

A sanitation advocacy story from Ado LGA, Benue State.

What changed

Sustained engagement helped move sanitation from advocacy conversation to institutional action.

The story documents engagement with WASH actors, town halls across 85 communities, support to sanitation actors and the supply of 1,700 Sato Pan Smart Toilets. Ado LGA subsequently declared a state of emergency on open defecation and inaugurated a sanitation taskforce.

The pathway from communication to institutional response.

This story demonstrates advocacy communications, government engagement, community mobilisation and impact storytelling.
