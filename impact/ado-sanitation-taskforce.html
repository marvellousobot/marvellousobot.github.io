FIELD NOTE / IMPACT

When advocacy becomes institutional action

A sanitation advocacy story from Ado LGA, Benue State.

THE HUMAN STORY

Development work becomes more visible when we understand the people, systems and decisions behind it.

01 / The context

Sustained engagement with WASH actors and communities created a pathway from sanitation advocacy to institutional response.

The project record documents town halls across 85 communities, support to sanitation actors and the supply of 1,700 Sato Pan Smart Toilets. Ado LGA subsequently declared a state of emergency on open defecation and inaugurated a sanitation taskforce.

02 / Project evidence

  • Town halls across 85 communities
  • 1,700 Sato Pan Smart Toilets supplied
  • Ado LGA declared a state of emergency on open defecation
  • A sanitation taskforce was inaugurated

These are project and institutional developments. They are not presented as individual outputs or as proof of sole causation.

03 / My contribution

Making the work visible and meaningful.

My contribution is positioned in the communications and advocacy work that supported community engagement, stakeholder mobilisation and the documentation of the change. The available record does not establish that my work alone caused the local government’s decisions or the reported supply figures.

04 / What this demonstrates

Communication that connects people, evidence and change.
