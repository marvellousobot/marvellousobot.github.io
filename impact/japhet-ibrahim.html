One Entrepreneur, 220+ Toilets

A human story from Kwali Area Council about sanitation entrepreneurship and community action.

What changed

Training and market opportunity helped one sanitation entrepreneur expand his business while contributing to local sanitation access.

Japhet Ibrahim moved into toilet sales and construction, promoted improved toilets and constructed toilets for a Primary Healthcare Centre that previously had none. The story reports that he had sold over 220 toilets.

Making systems-level work tangible through one person's experience.

You interviewed Japhet as Communications Officer and turned his experience into an impact story.
